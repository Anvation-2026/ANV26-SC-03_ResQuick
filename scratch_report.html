ResQuick AI — Project Technical Report
Smart Disaster Response, GIS Ingress Routing & Mathematical Resource Prioritization
System: DisasterIQ Municipal Core Date: October 2026 Document Type: Official Hackathon / Competition Technical Specification

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

During severe urban weather emergencies, cloudbursts, and flash floods, civic control rooms are overwhelmed by thousands of disconnected emergency calls. Operators face severe bottlenecks: they lack real-time visibility into which roads are submerged, struggle to communicate with citizens who do not speak English, and have no objective mathematical methodology to determine which trapped citizens or critical hospitals require immediate tactical resources.

ResQuick AI resolves this life-or-death crisis by providing a dual-portal ecosystem (Citizen Assistance Portal + Municipal Commander Command Center). It fuses browser-native multi-language speech recognition, automated NLP semantic translation to English, high-performance GIS street mapping with alternative ingress corridors, and a 5-factor dynamic mathematical risk formula that auto-ranks disaster zones and dynamically redistributes emergency resources in real-time.

4. Mathematical Risk Prioritization Model

Rather than relying on arbitrary operator decisions, ResQuick AI implements a formal Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) risk scoring engine. Every sector is continuously evaluated against five objective weighted parameters:

Risk Score = (0.30 × S) + (0.25 × P) + (0.20 × I) + (0.15 × A) + (0.10 × C)
Variable Parameter Name Weight Metric & Evaluation Criteria
S Hazard Severity 30% Physical depth of standing water (0–2m), building structural failure rate, fire flame velocity.
P Population Exposure 25% Count and demographic density of civilians, elderly, and patients trapped within the hazard polygon.
I Infrastructure Damage 20% Substation immersion, metro corridor shutdown, telecommunication tower collapse risk.
A Accessibility Difficulty 15% Degree of physical ingress blockage, culvert breaches, fallen trees, and debris obstruction.
C Critical Facility Threat 10% Proximity to lifeline facilities (hospitals, trauma units, oxygen supplies, drinking water plants).

Priority Score Tiers & Automated Action Rules

  • Score 80 – 100: CRITICAL — Immediate NDRF Tactical Water Squad dispatch, trauma ambulance rerouting, automated Siren Alert.
  • Score 60 – 79: HIGH — Fire & Rescue units deployed, heavy earthmovers tasked, alternative ingress routes opened.
  • Score 40 – 59: MODERATE — Municipal engineering dewatering pumps deployed, traffic diversions active.
  • Score < 40: LOW / WATCH — Continuous surveillance, preventative drain clearance, baseline watch.

6. Hackathon / Presentation Viva Questions & Ready Answers

Q1: What is the core innovation of ResQuick AI compared to Google Maps or Dial 112?
Answer: Commercial consumer maps only report slow traffic; they do not know that a 1.4-meter flood surge has cut off an ICU hospital approach, nor do they communicate with municipal departments. Dialing 112 creates isolated call logs where operators manually guess priorities. ResQuick AI dynamically aggregates multi-language citizen reports, applies an objective 5-factor mathematical risk model, ranks city sectors, and computes clear emergency ingress corridors to bypass blocked corridors.
Q2: How does the system handle citizens speaking in regional languages while officers need English?
Answer: When in distress, citizens must be able to speak their native mother tongue. ResQuick AI uses the HTML5 Web Speech Recognition API configured with regional dialect locales (such as kn-IN for Kannada or hi-IN for Hindi). The transcribed speech passes through a real-time semantic NLP translation engine that renders standardized administrative English in the Commander Admin Portal, eliminating linguistic barriers during crises.
Q3: How do you prevent fake, prank, or duplicate incident submissions?
Answer: ResQuick AI employs a three-tier defense: First, browser GPS bounding verifies that reports originate from real locations. Second, our spatial clustering engine aggregates complaints within a 500-meter radius into a single consolidated incident. Third, our client-side photo forensics check verifies natural lighting, structural fracture realism, and pixel continuity to eliminate AI-generated fakes.
Q4: What happens if an area suddenly deteriorates, such as a culvert breach?
Answer: We demonstrate this live using the Simulate Escalation Engine. When cloudburst conditions worsen in Indiranagar, its score instantly jumps from 82 to 94+. The algorithm promotes Indiranagar to Priority #1, sounds an automated audio alarm, and executes automated resource reallocation—dynamically diverting trauma ambulances and heavy earthmovers from lower-risk depots directly to the hospital corridor.
Q5: How does the system function if internet connectivity is lost during a disaster?
Answer: ResQuick AI is built with an offline-first resilient architecture: map vectors and baseline perimeters are cached locally in browser storage, incident drafts are saved locally until a connection resumes, and an integrated SMS broadcast gateway allows commanders to dispatch mass emergency advisories over basic 2G/GSM cellular networks.
